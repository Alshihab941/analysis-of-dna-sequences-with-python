# Resources and References

## Biopython
Biopython is a collection of freely available Python tools for computational molecular biology and bioinformatics.

https://biopython.org/

## Biopython SeqIO
`Bio.SeqIO` provides an interface for reading and writing biological sequence files, including FASTA.

https://biopython.org/wiki/SeqIO

## Biopython Tutorial and Cookbook
Useful background on sequence objects, sequence input/output, FASTA files, and sequence processing.

https://biopython.org/DIST/docs/tutorial/Tutorial-1.50.pdf

## Biopython paper
Cock, P. J. A., et al. (2009). Biopython: freely available Python tools for computational molecular biology and bioinformatics. *Bioinformatics, 25*(11), 1422–1423.

https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btp163

## Shannon entropy
Shannon, C. E. (1948). A Mathematical Theory of Communication. *Bell System Technical Journal, 27*, 379–423 and 623–656.

https://people.math.harvard.edu/~ctm/home/text/others/shannon/entropy/entropy.pdf

## FASTA background
Pearson, W. R., & Lipman, D. J. (1988). Improved tools for biological sequence comparison. *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences, 85*(8), 2444–2448.

https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.85.8.2448
